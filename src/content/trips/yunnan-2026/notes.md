## Food on plane

For all four flights, we had meals catered, given it was a full fare flight. I was pleasantly surprised at the variety and presentation of the food. I would actually look forward to the food they provide. Definitely worth the price.

## Budget

The entire 10 day trip cost each of us under $1100 SGD, flights and accommodation included. Accommodation in China is generally not expensive, even for good quality hotels. The hotel in Lijiang was definitely the most worthy in my opinion.

## Tips on hiking

When going on hiking trips, I recommend bringing a proper hiking bag instead of carrying luggage around, unless you are planning to leave it at a hotel to retrieve it afterwards. Like any other travel trips, knowing what clothes to bring is important, especially if only bringing hiking bags.

Knee conditioning is also important especially during the descent, where load on the knees are up to a few times your weight and your bag.

For my trip, I brought a fleece jacket which I didn't actually need, since the weather there was only around 10-20°C. I could have lightened my bag or used the extra space for other necessities.

Bag-to-body-weight ratio is an extremely important factor to consider when planning for a hike. It is generally recommended for the bag not to exceed 10-20% of body weight.

As for food, surprisingly, I did not feel very hungry despite the long hours of the hike. We only brought some 鲜花饼 as snacks to fill our stomach.

## Navigating in China

Almost all of our payments and buying of tickets were done through WeChat. Didi, through WeChat for ride hailing, ‘乘车码’ for metro, ‘高铁管家’ for booking of high speed rail tickets, which allow buying of tickets two weeks in advance. Most QR code there can be scanned with WeChat. Passports are usually required for identification during check-in or boarding of trains, so have them conveniently but safely with you.
