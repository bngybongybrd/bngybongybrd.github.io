This was my first trip out of South East Asia without my parents. It was an idea that spun up during a late night Discord call with my secondary school friends. I was yearning to go anywhere overseas after not travelling for quite a while and it so happened that Quentin was also in the call and wanted to travel somewhere as well. He initially had Taiwan in mind but we finally decided on Yunnan after finding out that the flight tickets there were more favourable.

Halfway planning the trip, we chanced upon Yubeng - a popular hiking spot located on a mountain village, after I suggested we do some hiking as a follow-up to my previous hiking experience at Bukhansan, Korea.

Before the trip, we met to buy hiking gear at Decathlon, which included these hiking shoes that I highly recommend. We copped our hiking sticks and socks on TaoBao.
