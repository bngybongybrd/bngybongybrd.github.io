Today was a chiller day. We woke up almost noon, and headed out for brunch at a nearby restaurant.

![](media/day-09/brunch.webp)
![](media/day-09/mushrooms.webp "Wild mushroom details")

We went off to find a place for scooter rentals, which took quite some time, almost giving up halfway. Weather was pleasant, and after a while of riding, we reached the outskirts. We took some detours along the way and stopped alongside some manmade scenic spot. There were people having their wedding photoshoot there. We left after realising there was payment for entry, and continued for 玉湖村 (Jade Lake village).

It was rather underwhelming after seeing so many tourists and seeing it being commercialised. I had hoped it would be more of a less known tourist spot. Regardless, we paid and got a ride up to the actual village, which was more or less a small version of the other ancient villages we visited.
