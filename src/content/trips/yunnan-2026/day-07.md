Today’s hike is to Sacred Waterfall (神瀑). Early morning, about 6am, I woke up to catch the 日照金山, which translates to sunlight illuminating golden mountain. Unfortunately, we went to Yubeng during the rainy season, so there were a lot of clouds blocking the peak of the mountain.

![](media/day-07/dog.webp "3 dogs accompanied me while I was walking down to Lower Yubeng.")
![](media/day-07/painting.webp)

![](media/day-07/timelapse.mp4)

After the sunrise, I headed to a nearby lodge to get breakfast while waiting for Quentin to arrive. I ordered seconds with him when he arrived.

![](media/day-07/breakfast.webp)

After breakfast, we slowly made our way to the start of the hike. The Sacred Waterfall trail was supposed to be the easiest trail among all trails in Yubeng, with Sacred Lake being the most challenging (we didn't do this trail). I initially thought it was relatively easy during the first hour or so, but nearing the waterfall was much more challenging than I thought.
