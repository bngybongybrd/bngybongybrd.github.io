Today was the hike to Ice Lake (冰湖). Can't remember what happened but I probably woke up earlier to try to catch the sunrise, but went back to our room since it was too late. Either way Quentin and I got ready and headed out to find a place to have our breakfast. The first store didn’t have some things on the menu so we left and entered another store nearby. They had beef noodle and fried rice for breakfast. We got both. I got another char siew bao afterwards. I realised most of the food stores here also doubled as lodging, as we saw some guests, if I'm not mistaken.

On the way back, there was a side route we wanted to try. We saw a couple at the junction unsure whether to proceed as well. After talking through our equipment (a torchlight in case it got dark) and the elevation of the trail, we decided to walk on for a bit. After about 10-15 minutes, we realised the elevation was quite steep. The husband was worried his wife wouldn’t be able to make it through the elevation and so the couple decided it wasn’t worth the risk and turned back toward the village via the original trail. Quentin and I decided it was best to follow them. Just a few minutes after we turned back, we found Quentin’s torchlight lying on the path. It was our only proper light source apart from our phones. The wife joked that it was probably an act of god that we’d turn back when we did.
